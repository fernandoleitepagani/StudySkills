# Didactic style guide

Simple, professional, and easy to understand.

The reader is a student who may be seeing the material for the first time. Optimize for comprehension and use.

## Voice

- Plain language.
- Short sentences.
- Active voice.
- Define jargon on first use.
- Prefer examples and analogies over abstract definitions.
- Explain why something matters before listing details.
- Do not write walls of text.

## Structure

Every document:

1. Cover: title, course, date, "StudySkills".
2. How to use this document.
3. Big picture and learning objectives.
4. Topic sections.
5. Recap: five things to remember.
6. Next steps.

Every topic section:

1. Key idea in one sentence.
2. Explanation.
3. Example or analogy.
4. Common mistake.
5. Check yourself question.

## Layout

- One idea per section.
- Generous margins and whitespace.
- Consistent heading hierarchy.
- Page break before major sections.
- Header and footer with course, document type, and page number.
- Table of contents for documents longer than eight pages.
- Tables for schedules, comparisons, and formulas.
- Callout boxes for definitions, tips, and warnings.
- Numbered steps for procedures.
- No decorative clip art.

## Typography

- Headings: bold, high contrast.
- Body: 11 to 12 pt, 1.3 line height.
- Maximum 80 characters per line where the format allows.
- Use a system sans-serif: Arial, Calibri, or Helvetica.
- Do not bundle fonts.

## Color

- Navy `#1F3A5F` for headings and structure.
- Amber `#E8A317` for emphasis only.
- Light gray `#F5F7FA` for callout backgrounds.
- Text `#1A1A1A`.
- Ensure high contrast.
- Never use color as the only way to convey meaning.

## PDF

- A4 or Letter, consistent.
- Print-safe margins.
- Page numbers.
- Contents page for long documents.
- No full-page background colors.

## DOCX

- Use real Word heading styles.
- Use editable tables.
- Keep the same visual hierarchy as the PDF.
- Avoid text boxes that are hard to edit.
- Include a cover page and a contents list.

## PPTX

- One idea per slide.
- Maximum six bullets per slide.
- Large readable text.
- Consistent title position.
- Speaker notes for extra explanation.
- No crowded diagrams.

## Accessibility

- High contrast.
- Descriptive headings.
- Alt text for meaningful images.
- Do not rely on color alone.
- Keep diagrams readable when printed in grayscale.

## Language

- PT-BR: natural Brazilian Portuguese.
- EN: clear international English.
- Keep quotes from source materials in their original language.
- Use consistent terminology within a document.

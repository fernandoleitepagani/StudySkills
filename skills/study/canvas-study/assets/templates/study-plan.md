# Study plan template

Use this structure when generating `study-plan.<format>`.

## Cover

- Course name
- "Study plan"
- Date range
- "StudySkills"

## How to use this plan

One short paragraph. Explain the weekly rhythm, the checkpoints, and how to adjust if a week slips.

## Deadline and time budget

- Exam or target date
- Weeks available
- Hours per week
- Total study hours

## Session routine

1. Warm up with recall.
2. Read or review one topic.
3. Do practice questions.
4. Explain the topic in your own words.
5. Schedule the next review.

## Review checkpoints

List the dates and the topics to revisit.

## Final week

A short plan for the last week before the exam or deadline.

## Next steps

Three concrete actions for today.
